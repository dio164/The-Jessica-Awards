# THE JESSICA AWARDS — CHAPTER XXIII
## A Season of Her: The Jessica Edition (November 14, 2026)

An interactive royal awards ceremony experience designed for Jessica's 23rd birthday sleepover.

### Visual Design System
- **Theme**: Vintage Royal Correspondence × Romantic Rococo Princess × Pink Heirloom Stationery
- **Colors**: Pink dominant, dusty rose, blush, ivory cream, antique gold, and burgundy ink
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond, Great Vibes cursive, EB Garamond serif, and Unifraktur blackletter
- **Motifs**: 12 custom Royal SVG insignias, wax seals with "XXIII", botanical filigrees, pearl dividers, and floating rose petals & gold stardust

### Ceremony Flow (Strictly Manual Advancement — Zero Autoplay)
- **Opening Screen**: Greet the guests, setup or review registry, click **"ENTER THE ROYAL COURT"**.
- **Stage A — Category Reveal**: Reveals the royal order, custom insignia, citation, and waits for **"REVEAL THE NOMINEES"**.
- **Stage B — Nominee Reveal**: Shows each courtier on an individual Rococo framed card with subtle golden spotlight, waiting for **"AND THE WINNER IS..."**.
- **Stage C — Winner Reveal**: Unseals the sovereign decree, triggers royal fanfare chimes, celebration rose petal shower, and crowns the winner. Waits for **"NEXT ROYAL AWARD"**.
- **Closing Ball**: Concludes Chapter XXIII with **"THE ROYAL COURT HAS SPOKEN"** and benediction **"LONG LIVE THE QUEENS OF CHAPTER XXIII"**.

---

### Presenter Controls & Keyboard Hotkeys
Jessica can operate the presentation directly from her keyboard:
- `Spacebar` or `→` (Right Arrow): Advance to next stage / step
- `←` (Left Arrow): Return to previous step / award
- `F`: Toggle Fullscreen Presentation Mode (optimized for TV / large display)
- `M`: Toggle Royal Sound Synthesizer (Fanfare chimes & drum roll; muted by default)
- `Esc`: Exit fullscreen or dismiss modal dialogs

---

### Chancery & Guest Registry
Click the **Curate Nominees & Winners** button (or slider icon in bottom HUD) to:
- Add, edit, or remove nominees per category
- Crown the designated winner with a single click
- Auto-save to browser `localStorage` across page refreshes
- Export / import the guest registry as JSON
- Optional 1-click **"Load Royal Court Demo"** to test with sample names
